Terms of use

This utility is designed for the account owner's personal backup administration.

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Permitted use

Homelab Backup is not an open public service. Access to its Google Drive integration is restricted to the authorised owner of the connected account. It is intended to store and verify backup archives created by systems the operator administers.

Backups and recovery

Backups are provided as an operational safeguard, without any guarantee of availability, suitability or successful restoration. The operator is responsible for protecting sensitive archives, reviewing scheduled job failures, testing restorations and maintaining appropriate security and legal authorisation for backed-up data.

Google integration

Use of Google Drive is subject to Google's applicable terms. Authorisation can be revoked in Google Account settings. Revocation prevents future uploads until authorisation is restored, but does not automatically delete files already stored in Drive.

Privacy

See the privacy policy for details on the Google data the utility accesses, how it is used, and retention settings.
